# Privacy policy (draft)

> **Draft, not legal advice.** Written from what the app's code does as of 2026-11-23. Needs Martin's sign-off, a
> contact address and an effective date before it is published.

## The short version

Show Up keeps your workout tracking on your device. You do not need an account. There are no ads and no analytics.

## What stays on your device

Everything you track: the programs you started, the days you crossed off (including the X you drew), bonus
workouts, notes, results, reminders, your own schedules and your settings. It is stored in the app's own storage
and removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

## What leaves your device, and when

| When | What | Who receives it |
|---|---|---|
| You sign in (optional) | Your email address, to send you a one-time code | Supabase, our hosting provider |
| You are signed in and have Pro | The tracking data listed above, so your other devices can show it | Supabase |
| The app checks for catalog updates | A request for the workout catalog; no account and no identifier | Supabase |
| You buy or restore Pro | The purchase | Google Play, the App Store or the Microsoft Store |
| You have the Android or iOS app | Your purchase status, tied to an anonymous id, or to your account id once you sign in | RevenueCat |

Reminders are scheduled on your device. Their text never leaves it.

## What we do not do

No advertising, no analytics or usage tracking, no selling or sharing of data, no access to contacts, location,
photos, health data or the microphone.

## Deleting your data

- On the device: uninstall the app or clear its data.
- Your account and the data synced to it: write to **[contact address — to be filled in]** from the email address of
  the account. Deleting an account from inside the app is not built yet.

## Children

Show Up is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

This page changes when the app starts handling data differently. The date at the top says when.
