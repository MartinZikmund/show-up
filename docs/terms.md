## What Show Up is

A workout tracker. It shows the published schedule of a workout program and lets you cross off what you did. It
contains no workout videos and no instructions, and it is not a medical device. Talk to a doctor before starting an
exercise program, and stop if something hurts.

## Independent app

Show Up is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Bodi (formerly Beachbody) or
any program creator. Program names are trademarks of their respective owners and are used only to identify the
workout schedules they describe. You need your own access to the workouts themselves.

## Calendars

Each calendar is checked against the program's official calendar, or against two independent sources where there
is no official one; the app says which. A calendar can still be wrong. If you find a mistake, report it and we will
fix it; a running program keeps the version it started with unless you choose to update.

## Show Up Pro

Pro is a subscription or a one-time purchase, bought through Google Play, the App Store or the Microsoft Store. A
subscription renews until you cancel it in your store account. Refunds follow the store's rules. If Pro ends,
nothing you tracked is deleted: extra programs and your own schedules become read-only until Pro is back.
