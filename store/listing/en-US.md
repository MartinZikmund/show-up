# Show Up: Workout Calendar

## Short description

Cross off every workout on a paper-style calendar. Don't break the chain.

## Description

Show Up is the calendar on your fridge, in your pocket. Pick a workout program, and every session you finish gets crossed off with a marker: you can draw the X yourself. The row of crosses is the point. You see what you did, what you skipped, and the streak you don't want to break.

Show Up tracks. It does not stream. You bring the workouts; it keeps you honest.

WHAT YOU GET
• Ready-made calendars for 21 home workout programs, including the schedules of Insanity, P90X, 21 Day Fix, Focus T25 and Couch to 5K, each checked against the official calendar, or against two independent sources where there is no official one.
• Days with two workouts: cross them off one at a time, and the X lands when the day is done.
• Bonus workouts: log a walk, a ride or an extra session without touching your program.
• A reminder that names today's workout.
• Notes and results: write down your Fit Test numbers and see them side by side.
• A small celebration when you finish a week, a phase, or the whole program.
• Missed a few days? Shift the schedule instead of starting over.
• Works offline. No account needed.

SHOW UP PRO
• Run several programs at once.
• Build your own schedule, week by week.
• More reminders.
• Your full history, and statistics: workouts per week, a consistency heatmap, how your programs compare.
• Marker colours.

Everything that keeps you accountable is free: crossing off, streaks, shifting the schedule, finishing a program.
