# Marker sounds

`marker-1.wav`, `marker-2.wav`, `marker-3.wav` and `marker-tick.wav` are synthesized from scratch by
[`tools/generate-marker-sounds.py`](../../../../tools/generate-marker-sounds.py) (Python stdlib only:
band-pass swept noise plus a faint triangle squeak, 44.1 kHz mono 16-bit). No third-party
recordings or samples are involved.

They are released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): do whatever
you like with them. Regenerate with `python tools/generate-marker-sounds.py`.
